`mod` = `slow_mod * s + holo_mod * H` of the FULL arm, with the coefficients read from the medium configuration (0.3 and 0.5); `mod_s` and `mod_H` its two parts; `mod_ring` = `slow_mod * s` of the hologram-off arm; `u_full`, `u_ring` the fast fields (their linear effect on the probe is removed by the paired baseline subtraction); `NULL` zeros. ## 9. Recommended next step

The slow field carries the shape information the probe can reach, but nothing so far shows how long it lasts, and the hologram (the only memory designed to persist) contributes little at 250 steps. The retention experiment can now be designed properly. It needs **new simulation** (about 20 to 30 minutes):

1. **Delays of 0, 250, 500, 1000 and 2000 steps**, with three lesions made at the end of the write phase: **hologram only** (fast and slow fields zeroed), **hologram plus slow field** (fast field zeroed), and **slow field only** (hologram off, fast field zeroed). The v9 to v11 lesions never separated the slow field from the fast one.
2. **Decode shapes from the modulation map** `0.3 s + 0.5 H` at the sensor-equivalent noise levels used here, and compare with the probe-based decoding at the same delays.
3. **Pre-registered prediction to test:** the slow field dominates at short delays and fades; the hologram's part, small at 250 steps, is the one that persists. If so, the hologram's R-squared should exceed the slow field's at long delays and plasticity extends memory. If both fade together, or the hologram never exceeds the noise floor, the plastic memory does not add to the field memory in this design, and the write rule needs redesigning (for example a reference-beam hologram).

---

## 10. Limitations

* **One medium, one configuration, two seeds** of 240 trials; the results agree across seeds.
* **The noise model is a calibration**, not the real probe path; conclusions about the probe rest on the effective-noise statistic and are qualitative.
* **Pooling to 361 values** and the random 24-value subset are choices; the pooled written map is a weak reference (about 0.45 for shape).
* **Only one delay (250 steps)** is examined; nothing here speaks about retention.
* **The probe's use of the map is a hypothesis**, consistent with the data but not separated from the alternative that it reads the fast field.
* **The verdict label depends on the noise assumption** (section 3).
